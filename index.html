Real-time heuristic threat engine · 100% client-side

Scan QR codes.
Expose phishing threats.

GarudaNetra decodes any QR code or URL and inspects it against a battery of security heuristics to reveal phishing and malware threats — with a clear risk score, plain-language explanations, and downloadable PDF reports. Everything runs locally in your browser; no link or image is ever uploaded.

What GarudaNetra does

QR Upload

Drag & drop or browse a QR image — it's decoded instantly, right in your browser.

Camera Scanning

Point your device camera at a QR code for automatic, live detection.

Risk Scoring

Every link gets a 0–100 risk score and a clear Safe / Suspicious / Dangerous verdict.

Threat Explanations

Risky links come with plain-language reasons and recommended actions.

PDF Reports

Download a branded threat report for any scan to keep or share.

100% Private

All analysis happens on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

Threat analysis workflow

Step 01

Capture

Upload, camera-scan, or paste a URL.

Step 02

Decode

The QR payload is extracted in-browser.

Step 03

Analyze

Weighted signals inspect the link.

Step 04

Score

A 0–100 risk score & verdict is built.

Step 05

Report

Explanations & a PDF threat report.

How Threats Are Scored

Each URL is evaluated against a set of weighted heuristics used by security teams to spot phishing. Matched signals add to a 0–100 risk score, which maps to a verdict:

0–34 Safe 35–64 Suspicious 65–100 Dangerous
